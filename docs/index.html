NEAR Intents blocked $50M of hacker money, then got hacked itself. Its volume kept pace with the market.

The bear caseA venue that can freeze funds isn't trustless. Users leave the first time it does.
The testIn one week: SHIELD blocked $50M tied to the Bitget hack, then a $3.8M exploit (funds returned next day).
The resultNo collapse. Volume kept pace with its model, built from rival venues and the market.
The catchIts summer momentum stopped. Established accounts pulled back. Zcash balances drained.
